# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, constructive project.

Be respectful, assume good intent, accept feedback gracefully and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publication of private Plex data are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository’s private contact channels.
